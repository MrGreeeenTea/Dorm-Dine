# Solution Elements

I resorted to taking another look at the questions from 01: design challenges. 
The recent discoveries suggest there will be an imbalance in roles. 
We will have more buyers than cooks. Which suggest excessive demand. 
To counter this it is our top priority to make our app as appaealing as possible, especially for the cooks. 

1. Cook first & cleanliness

   Most important is a clean working space for the cooks. To make sure everyone cleans after themselves we want to implement a feature that allows cook to upload a picture of the cleaned up kitchen after they used it.
   This will be time stamped to insure that if someone else used the kitchen in between the time until the next cook wants to use it, the first cook cannot be blamed for any dirt that was left AFTER he used the kitchen.
   We want to include Chek-in and Check-out pictures as a fuction, tho the check-in is fully optional and would only need to apply if a cook finds the kitchen dirty after someone else used it.

2. Anternative service and supply payments
    
   To support the cooks we want to give buyers an option to pay in other ways that dont include cash or paypal.
   The first option is a pre-determined service payment. The buyer can pay for a meal by cleaning up the kitchen and doing the dishes for the cook. We are also thinking about including an option to let buyers suggest a service to the cook but this is not decided yet.
   The second option is a supply payment. Cooks have the option to sell meals that are already made or to add a posting for a meal they want to prepare. Buyers can then essentially pre order those meals. As a payment option for those specific type of meals we want to include the possibility for buyers to provide ingredients from the list the cook submitted towards the proccess of making the meal. This might save the cook money and time.

4. Ecouraging rating system
   
   No ensure there can be no negative ratings that might make the cook feel like not wanting to make any more meals we are thinking about adding an option for buyers to not rate in the traditional star way but instead let them give a thumbs up if they liked the meal. There will be no thumbs down option.
   Because the target users are interested in a counter that displays the cooks expierence by showcasing how many meals they have sold we will add a feature like that to the cooks profile.
