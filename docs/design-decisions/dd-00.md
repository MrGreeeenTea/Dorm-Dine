# 00: How to attract Persona B to the platform

## Meta

Status
: Work in progress - **Decided** - Obsolete

Updated
: 23-06-2026

## Problem Statement

There are possibly not enough people who want to take the role of Persona B. Because of for example dirty dorm kitchens, additional extra effort and no assurance if there are enough Persona A interested in the food. How do we make it for people attractive?

## Decision

Due to shortage of time we decided to continue with our project for now with our original idea in mind while keeping options like incentives for Persona B in mind to attract them to the platform later when clearly see the issue. 

*Decision was taken by:* github.com/juliadhwr

## Regarded Options

Option 1: Keep the model unchanged despite risks
Option 2: Make the platform more attractive with incentives for Persona B

| Regarded Options | Option 1 | Option 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
|Pro | ✔️ Fast to implement, no extra changes and keeps our original idea of people cooking to be sustainable  | ✔️ Can make the Role of Persona B more attractive |
|Con | ❌ Continue the carrying the risks of the Persona B Problem | ❌ Incentives may increase costs, attract people that aim for profit and there is no guarantee that it would fix the problem |
