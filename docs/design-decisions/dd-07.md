# 07: Should the user see real photos of the food or Icons ?

## Meta

Status
: Work in progress - **Decided** - Obsolete

Updated
: 12-06-2026

## Problem Statement

Should available meals be represented with real food photos uploaded by users, or should we use standardized food icons to visually support the meal offers?
Our web application is designed to help students discover affordable home-cooked meals within their dormitory. Visual presentation plays an important role in making the meal overview easy to understand, attractive, and trustworthy.
Using real photos could make the offers feel more personal and realistic. However, it would also create additional effort for the cook, require image uploads, increase storage needs, and may lead to inconsistent visual quality. Some cooks may upload blurry, poorly lit, or inappropriate images, which would negatively affect the overall look and reliability of the platform.
Since our project focuses on a clear and simple UI, we need a visual solution that is easy to implement, consistent across all meals, and suitable for quick browsing.

## Decision

We decided to represent meal options with standardized food icons instead of real food photos.
Each meal offer can be visually supported by an icon that represents the type or category of the dish, for example pasta, rice, soup, salad, or dessert. This allows users to quickly recognize the general type of food without requiring every cook to upload a picture.
This approach keeps the user interface clean, consistent, and easy to maintain. It also avoids problems related to image quality, upload handling, storage, privacy, and moderation.
For the current state of the app, icons are sufficient to support the browsing experience while keeping the technical complexity low. Real food photos could still be considered as a future improvement once the core functionality of the platform is stable.

Decision was taken by: github.com/MrGreeeenTea (Lars Unger)

## Regarded options

We regarded two alternative options:

+ Use real food photos uploaded by users
+ Use standardized food icons for meal offers

| Criterion                  | Real food photos                                             | Standardized food icons                     |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Visual realism**             | ✔️ Meals look more personal and realistic                    | ❔ Icons only show the general food type     |
| **User effort**         | ❌ Users need to take and upload photos                       | ✔️ No image upload required                 |
| **UI consistency**            | ❌ Photos may differ in quality, size, and style              | ✔️ Consistent and clean visual appearance   |
| **Implementation effort**  | ❌ Requires upload handling, storage, and image validation    | ✔️ Easier to implement in the current state           |
| **Moderation**         | ❌ Inappropriate or low-quality images may need to be checked | ✔️ Less risk because icons are predefined   |
| **Performance**            | ❔ Larger image files may slow down loading                   | ✔️ Icons are lightweight and fast to load   |
| **Trust and transparency**     | ✔️ Real photos can show the actual meal                      | ❔ Icons are less specific than photos       |
| **current state suitability**        | ❌ Adds technical complexity early                            | ✔️ Fits the simple and focused MVP approach |
